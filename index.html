Read before using the plan: units, zones and source notes

Swimming units. The book leaves swimming distances unlabelled and designs them with yards in mind, while allowing the workouts to be done in any pool. This page preserves every original number. “yd / m” means use the numbers in yards in a yard pool, or in metres in a metric pool; these are not converted equivalent distances. Completing the same numbers in metres covers approximately 10% farther. No distance has been shortened or converted here.

Time and notation. Weekly times use hours:minutes. Workout times use minutes or seconds. Z1–Z5 mean the book’s sport-specific 80/20 intensity zones, not automatically your watch’s default zones. “n × (effort / recovery)” means repeat the entire pair n times, including the final recovery. A rest with no zone is passive rest as specified, not an invented Z1 segment.

Intensity balance. Low/high percentages are the original weekly values. The cycling and running appendices count recovery intervals of three minutes or less with the high-intensity block for intensity accounting, even when those recoveries are performed in Z1. The race-specific balance also allows for expected Zone X work. Do not recalculate these printed percentages from heart-rate time-in-zone alone.

Original sequence. Stage headings clarify the printed sequence without adding training. When an entry has no separate cooldown, none has been invented. Recovery sessions are single blocks. Empty cells preserve the original blank cells. The final Sunday is blank in the supplied table; a race workout or race duration has not been added.

Race-specific bricks. During the race-specific phase, do cycling and running workouts scheduled together on a weekend day back to back as one brick. Weekday bike/run combinations are optional bricks. Use these to practise pacing, nutrition and equipment.

Source discrepancies retained. Week 9 lists 7,350 swim units, although its three workout entries total 7,500. Week 12 lists 7,600, although its entries total 7,750. RTa1 is labelled 10 minutes, but its printed sequence totals 11 minutes. These original values remain visible, with notes beside the affected entries. Weekly cycle totals can round a half-minute workout to whole minutes.

Preparation. The book describes Level 2 for competitive athletes with prior triathlon experience or a strong history in one discipline. Before beginning, be comfortable swimming, cycling and running for one hour and completing six total aerobic training hours in a week.
